# DESIGN.md

## Identity
A dashboard for monitoring child nutrient status. Built for health workers and posyandu staff who track child growth and nutritional data.

## Personality
Calm and trustworthy. The UI should feel reliable and professional — never alarming, never playful. Users are making health decisions; the interface should reinforce their confidence.

## Palette
- **Primary:** Light blue — clean, medical-adjacent, non-aggressive
- **Avoid:** Dark, aggressive, or saturated accent colors that create visual noise
- Neutral backgrounds (white or very light grey); subtle borders rather than heavy dividers

## Typography
Clean sans-serif. Legible at small sizes. No decorative or display fonts. Hierarchy through weight and size, not style variation.

## Mood
Fresh and clean. Open whitespace. Nothing crowded. The data is the content; the chrome should recede.

## Three Dials (antislop)
- **ENERGY:** 2 — calm, not flat; slight visual distinction between interactive and static elements
- **RHYTHM:** 2 — consistent spacing grid, mild variation for hierarchy
- **MOTION:** 1 — minimal; transitions only where they aid comprehension (e.g. loading states), never decorative
